My process

Understand the problem

I start the design process with research, including surveys, user interviews, data analysis, and competitor benchmarking to discover user's problems and find improvement opportunities.

Ideate a solution

Next, I lead collaborative sessions with engineers and product managers to ideate innovative solutions. I then create low-fidelity mockups to visualize and define a high level solution.

Craft with excellence

I then transform the selected solution into a polished, high-fidelity prototype, focusing on usability, micro-interactions, accessibility, and compelling content.

Validate & test

The final step involves validating the solution through user testing. I use the discovered insights to polish the final design and deliver detailed specs to engineers for implementation.
